Why psychology in MARL?

Formula 1 drivers differ in lap-time consistency under pressure. Veterans stay composed after setbacks; rookies show wider variance. We encode that signal as per-agent experience from telemetry and a scalar psych_state that degrades after poor team rewards and recovers over time.

Appending psych_state to observations improves training consistency (Strategy Resilience Score) in cooperative MARL, even when raw return shifts only modestly.

Two evaluation regimes

  • Tabular Q-learning — sanity checks on discretized observations
  • MAPPO (RLlib PPO) — parameter-shared policies with joint observations

Reference

Inspired by psychology-aware MARL ideas and grounded in arXiv:2602.23056 (Feb 2026): behavior-aware signals can stabilize learning, not only maximize return.

F1PsychologyWrapper

Wraps simple_spread_v3 with two mechanisms:

1. Action noise — Gaussian perturbation scaled by inexperience:

a′i = ai + 𝒩(0, (1 − ei) · 0.1)

2. Psychological state ψi ∈ [0.5, 1.0]:

  • Team reward < −0.5 → ψ drops by 0.2 (floor 0.5)
  • Otherwise → ψ recovers by 0.05 (cap 1.0)

Psych-aware MAPPO runs append ψ to each agent observation via PsychStateObsWrapper.
